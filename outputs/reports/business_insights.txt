CUSTOMER CHURN ANALYSIS: BUSINESS INSIGHTS
================================================

DATASET OVERVIEW
Customers analyzed: 7,032
Customers churned: 1,869
Customers retained: 5,163
Overall churn rate: 26.58%

KEY FINDINGS
1. Churned customers averaged 17.98 months of tenure, compared with 37.65 months for retained customers.
2. Churned customers paid an average of $74.44 per month, compared with $61.31 for retained customers.
3. Retained customers had 19.67 more months of average tenure.
4. Churned customers paid $13.13 more per month on average.

RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Investigate early-tenure churn and test onboarding or customer-support improvements.
2. Review pricing, service usage, and perceived value among higher-charge customers.
3. Investigate high-churn contract and payment segments before designing targeted interventions.
4. Measure retention campaign outcomes against a suitable comparison group before scaling.

LIMITATIONS
These are observational findings and do not establish causation. Churn rates describe historical customers; they are not predictions of future churn.
Records with missing essential numeric values were excluded during cleaning.
